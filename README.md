## 1. When the machine doesn't understand you

**This is the current one** — https://maxderfisch.github.io/Powerpoint/

Essay presentation: how AI enforces Standard English Ideology, and how speech
recognition is quietly changing the way people pronounce English.

6 slides, 5 minutes of talking. Slide 6 is the essay itself and just stays on
screen at the end.

- the deck: [index.html](index.html)
- what to say: [essay-script.md](essay-script.md)
- backup: [AI-and-Standard-English.pdf](AI-and-Standard-English.pdf)

### Sources

- Word error rates: Koenecke et al., *Racial disparities in automated speech
  recognition*, PNAS (2020) — Stanford. Five systems tested (Amazon, Apple,
  Google, IBM, Microsoft). 19 % error rate for white speakers, 35 % for Black
  speakers.

## 2. English is a Thief

The earlier presentation, from 14 September —
https://maxderfisch.github.io/Powerpoint/english-is-a-thief.html

Where English words really come from: the family tree, who English borrowed
from, and the Korean words the Oxford English Dictionary added in 2021.

16 slides. On slide 3 you uncover the language names by clicking the blocks in
the bar (or keys 1–5, A for all).

- the deck: [english-is-a-thief.html](english-is-a-thief.html)
- what to say: [english-is-a-thief-script.md](english-is-a-thief-script.md)
- backup: [English-is-a-Thief.pdf](English-is-a-Thief.pdf)
- an older version: [backup.html](backup.html)

Presented by 허성윤 · 윤예원 · Max Aulinger.

### Sources

- Vocabulary numbers: Finkenstaedt & Wolff, *Ordered Profusion* (1973) — about
  80,000 words of the Shorter Oxford English Dictionary.
- Word origins: Oxford English Dictionary / Online Etymology Dictionary.
- 26 Korean words: Oxford English Dictionary update, September 2021.
- Old texts: West Saxon Gospels (c. 1000), Wycliffe Bible (c. 1390), King James
  Bible (1611).
- Proto-Indo-European homeland: the Pontic–Caspian steppe.
- Background: BBC / Open University, *The History of English in 10 Minutes*.
