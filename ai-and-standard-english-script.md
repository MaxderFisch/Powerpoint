# Speaking script — When the machine doesn't understand you

6 slides, 5 minutes. Slides 1 to 5 are the talk — about one minute each.
Slide 6 is just the essay, left on screen at the end.

## 1 — Title

Hello everyone.

A few days ago I tried to use the voice-to-text on my phone. It did not work.

I speak German with a Bavarian dialect, and the AI simply did not understand me.

That is where this essay started.

## 2 — It started with my own voice

I am German, but I speak a strong Bavarian dialect. So when I use voice-to-text,
my phone often writes nonsense.

For example, I say "I hob koa Zeit" — that is Bavarian for "I have no time".
And my phone writes "I hope Korea".

At first I thought the software was broken. But it is not. I just do not sound
like the people it learned from.

And then I thought: if this happens to me in German — what happens to English
speakers with an accent?

Because AI is in everything now. Whether we like it or not.

## 3 — It is not just a feeling

So I looked for studies, and I found one from Stanford, published in 2020.

They tested five speech recognition systems — Amazon, Apple, Google, IBM and
Microsoft. Same sentences, different speakers.

For white speakers, the software got 19 percent of the words wrong.
For Black speakers, 35 percent. Almost twice as many mistakes.

And non-native speakers hit exactly the same wall. If you have an accent, the
machine fails.

## 4 — The machine does not just listen. It decides.

Now, why does this change the language?

TikTok and YouTube use automatic captions to understand what your video is
about, and who to show it to.

If the caption is wrong, fewer people see you. Fewer views. Less money.

So creators are starting to change how they speak. They try to sound more
standard, just so the machine understands them.

That is the chain. And the machine never had to change at all.

## 5 — AI should learn our accents

This idea has a name: Standard English Ideology. The belief that one way of
speaking is the real English, and everything else is a mistake.

And it is now built into software we all use every day.

My essay has five parts.

*(go through the list quickly, don't read it word for word)*

My conclusion: English is an international language. So the AI has to learn our
accents — instead of forcing everyone into one single standard.

Thank you.
